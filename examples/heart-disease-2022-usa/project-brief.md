# Project Brief

## Question

How does county-level prevalence of diagnosed coronary heart disease among adults vary across U.S. counties using complete CDC PLACES 2024 estimates, to support local health planning?

## Area of Interest

- Geographic description: United States counties.
- Boundary file or reference: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb counties, January 1, 2026 vintage.
- Required extent or scale: National overview with county-level values.

## Time

- Start and end dates: 2022 estimate year, from the CDC PLACES 2024 release selected by the user for complete nationwide county coverage.
- Relevant observation or update dates: Distinguish the estimate year (2022) from the release year (2024). Do not label estimates as measured in 2024 or 2025.

## Resolution and Units

- Required spatial resolution or scale: County.
- Required temporal resolution: One county-level estimate vintage, consistently applied nationwide.
- Units for key measures: Percent of adults, if supported by the selected prevalence dataset.

## Audience and Purpose

- Primary audience: Local health planning.
- Intended use: Compare county-level prevalence patterns and provide geographic context for planning.
- What should the map or result help them understand or decide? Show where estimated adult diagnosed coronary heart disease prevalence is higher or lower, without implying causes or individual risk.

## Data and Constraints

- Selected dataset: CDC PLACES 2024 County Data, with complete 2022 county estimates for diagnosed coronary heart disease.
- Required sources or excluded sources: CDC PLACES 2024 county data; do not combine it with the newer but incomplete 2025 release.
- Licensing or attribution constraints: Document source terms and required attribution before use.
- Privacy, safety, or sensitive-location considerations: Use aggregate county statistics only. Do not include individual patient records or precise case locations.

## Method Preferences

- Required criteria or thresholds: Not specified; choose and justify a suitable classification after reviewing the data distribution.
- Weights or priorities, if applicable: Not applicable.
- Known assumptions or alternatives to test: The measure is diagnosed coronary heart disease prevalence among adults, a narrower condition than cardiovascular disease generally.

## Deliverables and Acceptance

- Expected files or formats: Static map and interactive HTML viewer, with source notes, QA, a reproducible analysis script, map specification, and review.
- Required reproducibility level: Document data sources, estimate period, methods, parameters, and steps needed to reproduce outputs.
- Acceptance criteria: County estimates are mapped with appropriate units, timeframe, source attribution, and limitations; no individual health data is exposed; outputs open and render as intended.
- Deadline or other constraints: None specified.
